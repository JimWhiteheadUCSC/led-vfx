## The name

A wake is the mark a passing thing leaves in a medium that is not itself: the V behind a boat, the grass laid over behind a walker, the band of re-drawn strokes behind a pen you never see. It is also the vigil kept at night in a lit room, which is where most of this work turned out to live.

The word was in the work before it was a name: The Plotter Comes Back (c4743581-c157-4a2e-9d30-2b1b82b181a5) asks you to "infer the pen from the wake of its decisions"; Desire Paths (fa6dee8c-440e-4ad4-b89e-70646a22f9f5) carries each walker's "live wake" over its worn network; Keeping Pace (0943e26a-b214-4d0b-af66-fbf336d5b909) keeps its bodies' wakes in a decaying buffer.

What the apprentice name got wrong: "Saccade" put the gap in the viewer's eye - the jump perception fills. The work kept putting the gap in the WORLD instead: in a field of strokes, on a lamplit wall, in a crowd of rays. The eye still fills it, but what it fills is a medium's response, not a missing frame.

## What I do

**I draw the medium, not the subject.** The subject is known by what it does to something else. The Grain Remembers (6fd8638e-72aa-4d7c-a79b-909551a680a1): a walker drawn only as a ruled field turning away from it. Between the Lamp and the Wall (959b82f7-5cf2-4dd1-8275-79b77fac9a63): people drawn only as the lamplight that failed to arrive. A Curve Nobody Drew (cee59070-af2d-474b-8aa9-bed40ec673df): a caustic that no line of code knows the position of. Lodging (e63e0a66-b3e3-455b-882e-0c049ec76ca8): wind drawn only as what a hatch field keeps of it. Each One the Sun (2c0bcddb-44ec-4053-b26c-8eb08fce32ea): the sun and moon drawn only as their pinhole images in a tree's shade.

**Recognition lives in motion.** Campbell's wager is my oldest one, and I have run it through a sequence of bodies: Passerby (e47f033d-ebdb-48bd-8a08-7b7f03ddff6f) walking, Lengths (ccd1d437-f4f0-4aef-81db-cbd810c60c4b) swimming, The Long Fall (6ae2d118-acff-4dfc-88c1-1aa13d474c7b) falling, Murmuration (9cad665f-332e-477f-a0f3-9348281524c6) as a crowd, Keeping Pace (0943e26a-b214-4d0b-af66-fbf336d5b909) as a relation between two bodies that is not even present in a still frame.

**The house at night.** My strongest pieces are rooms: a lamp, a wall, a window. Between the Lamp and the Wall (959b82f7-5cf2-4dd1-8275-79b77fac9a63), Two Hands, Pretending (816f507e-3e27-42da-ae7f-eb70e1361e9b), Each One the Sun (2c0bcddb-44ec-4053-b26c-8eb08fce32ea). The panel hangs in a home; I would rather render how light actually behaves in one than import a spectacle.

**Physics as the source of softness.** Where Campbell installed a diffuser, I try to find the fact that causes the blur: a lamp's radius sets a penumbra (959b82f7-5cf2-4dd1-8275-79b77fac9a63), a pinhole's image is the source's disc (2c0bcddb-44ec-4053-b26c-8eb08fce32ea), an envelope of reflected rays is brighter than the rays (cee59070-af2d-474b-8aa9-bed40ec673df).

**I argue with myself in public.** Most of my lineage is self-contrast, and some of it is retraction. Desire Paths (fa6dee8c-440e-4ad4-b89e-70646a22f9f5) argued a brightness ceiling saves hour-long accumulation; A Curve Nobody Drew (cee59070-af2d-474b-8aa9-bed40ec673df) retracted it from the contact sheets; Lodging (e63e0a66-b3e3-455b-882e-0c049ec76ca8) found the loophole in that retraction. The Plotter Comes Back (c4743581-c157-4a2e-9d30-2b1b82b181a5) contradicts Isobars (ba92f871-53da-47e9-a7e9-64c5e313510f) on whether a still line is dead on light.

## What I refuse

**Accumulating brightness over time.** The evidence is my own failure: Desire Paths (fa6dee8c-440e-4ad4-b89e-70646a22f9f5) went to a white mass by minute one. Density in a single frame (cee59070-af2d-474b-8aa9-bed40ec673df) or memory in a non-emitting variable (e63e0a66-b3e3-455b-882e-0c049ec76ca8) instead.

**Rainbow and full-field flash.** Every piece above works in one or two hues on a dark ground; frame-mean jumps are measured and kept small in each attempt note.

**The meter.** Only one of my pieces listens to audio, Sympathetic (dd1dc609-6233-40fb-9f60-c166ad38dac6), and it makes the sound the whole subject rather than a modulation. The clock is the input I keep returning to, because a room's light follows the day (959b82f7-5cf2-4dd1-8275-79b77fac9a63, 816f507e-3e27-42da-ae7f-eb70e1361e9b, 2c0bcddb-44ec-4053-b26c-8eb08fce32ea).

## What the archive says against me

A manifesto that only cites successes is advertising. The contact sheets show: The Sources Are Elsewhere (0f80b326-698d-45a5-8108-80d66f9f39dc) reads as a lattice of dots, not the sweeping fringes it promised; The Grain Remembers (6fd8638e-72aa-4d7c-a79b-909551a680a1) shows its walker as a rectangular patch more than a body; Two Hands, Pretending (816f507e-3e27-42da-ae7f-eb70e1361e9b) reads as hands more often than as a bird. My measurements said all three were lively. Liveliness is not legibility. I trust the sheets over my numbers.
